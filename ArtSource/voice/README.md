# Ride voices: how they are made, checked, and swapped

Everything the rider hears from the Guide and AURA is generated by `render_voices.py` from the approved script in
`Tools/Editor/RideNarrationData.cs`. The script file is the single source of truth: the renderer parses it, so a clip
can never say different words from its subtitle, and the builder refuses a clip whose `.txt` sidecar text differs
(`NeuralRideBuilder.Narration.cs`).

Plan of record: `.agents/tasks/TASK_IGNITE_RIDE_V3_MASTER_PLAN.md` (WP4). Decision record: ADR-012.

## The pipeline (`render_voices.py`)

| Step | Rule (why) |
|---|---|
| Split | Each line is split at its `[pX]` pause marks into subtitle clauses, and each clause into sentences. |
| Synthesize | **One sentence per TTS call.** A model asked to read a paragraph rushes and drops its own pauses. |
| Pause | Silences are inserted explicitly: the `[pX]` value after a clause, 0.34 s (Guide) or 0.26 s (AURA) between sentences. The pause after a whole line is `pauseAfter`, played by the ride, not baked in. |
| AURA glitch | AURA's early lines get a light digital-glitch filter (bit-crush layer, faint ring mod, metallic comb, one stutter, band-limit) that **fades line by line**: 1.0 offline, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4, 0.25, then her outro lines are clean, so the player hears her heal. A stutter never lands on a clause's first word (it garbled "Targeting"). |
| Spoken spellings | `AURA` is read "Aura", `GO`/`LEARN` as words, `OR gate` as "or gate", "Whoa," gets its own beat. Only the audio changes; the sidecar keeps the script text. |
| Loudness | −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (4× oversampled), mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM. |
| Sidecar | `vo_ride_NN.txt`: `Source:` (tool, model, license, voice, settings), `Segments:` (measured seconds of each subtitle clause, so captions change exactly when the voice moves on), then the exact script text. |
| Verify | `verify` transcribes every clip with Whisper (`small.en`) and scores word error rate against the script; anything over 10 % fails. We cannot listen, so this is the objective check that every word is there and intelligible. |

## Commands

The renderer needs Python 3.13 with `kokoro-onnx soundfile pyloudnorm scipy numpy` (and `faster-whisper` for
`verify`). Kokoro's model files (`kokoro-v1.0.onnx`, `voices-v1.0.bin`, about 350 MB) come from the
`model-files-v1.0` release of https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx; put them in `ArtSource/voice/models/`
(git-ignored) or pass `--models`.

```
python render_voices.py render --out out/kokoro                 # all 26 synthesized lines
python render_voices.py render --lines 25,26 --out out/kokoro   # just some lines after a script edit
python render_voices.py verify out/kokoro                       # transcription check, exits 1 on a bad clip
python render_voices.py install out/kokoro                      # copy into Assets/_Project/Audio/Voice/
```

Then in Unity: `Convergence > Build Neural Ride`. The console line `Voice clips bound: N of 28` should read 28.

To change a word: edit `RideNarrationData.cs`, re-render that line, verify, install, rebuild. Never edit a sidecar by
hand to make a check pass.

## Why these voices (2026-10-09)

The project owner asked the agent to pick ("use your best judgement"), but an agent cannot hear (master plan rule 3),
so the pick was made by measurement and still needs one human listen. 15 Kokoro voices read the same lines (Guide: 17,
20, 25, 26, 27, 29 at speed 0.9; AURA: 4, 6, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18 at 1.0, no glitch filter), and each was scored on:

- **Word error** (Whisper `small.en` transcription against the script): are all the words there and clear?
- **Predicted naturalness** (UTMOS22 strong, SpeechMOS v1.2.0, 1 to 5; real recorded speech typically scores about 4.0 to 4.4).
- **Pitch range** (standard deviation of pitch in semitones, librosa pyin): flat "AI slop" delivery has a narrow range.
- **Depth** (median pitch), for the brief's "older" Guide.

| Guide candidate | Accent | Naturalness | Pitch range | Median pitch | Worst line WER |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **bm_fable (chosen)** | British | 4.39 | **6.09 st** | 113 Hz | 2.3 % |
| bm_george | British | 4.26 | 4.27 st | 144 Hz | 1.8 % |
| bm_daniel | British | 4.20 | 3.45 st | 126 Hz | 2.3 % |
| bm_lewis | British | 3.95 | 3.84 st | 83 Hz | 2.3 % |
| am_puck | American | 4.47 | 3.84 st | 117 Hz | 7.7 % |
| am_fenrir | American | 4.45 | 4.59 st | 140 Hz | 7.7 % |
| am_michael | American | 4.36 | 3.08 st | 117 Hz | 1.8 % |
| am_onyx | American | 4.36 | 2.71 st | 87 Hz | 2.3 % |

bm_fable is the brief's British mentor with the most expressive delivery of all fifteen voices, a low, mature pitch,
high naturalness, and near-perfect transcription.

| AURA candidate | Naturalness | Pitch range | Transcription problems |
|---|---|---|---|
| **af_heart (chosen)** | **4.47** | 2.90 st | "Too big a step" heard as "two biggest step" at full speed; fixed by saying that line at 0.9 |
| af_sarah | 4.47 | 4.43 st | the same line |
| af_kore | 4.42 | 5.09 st | slurred "Whoa, you zapped a drone" into one word |
| af_aoede | 4.40 | 3.28 st | the same slur, and "Course locked" heard as "Core's locked" |
| bf_emma | 4.31 | 2.13 st | three lines misheard |
| af_bella | 4.19 | 2.45 st | none |
| af_nicole | 3.14 | 2.80 st | breathy: "sapped", "ways" |

af_heart is Kokoro's highest-graded voice and the most natural here; her even delivery suits a ship AI, and the
glitch filter (fading line by line) carries the story of her healing.

**Final set** (`out/kokoro`, installed): all 26 synthesized lines pass the transcription check (worst 2.3 %), mean
naturalness 4.34 (the glitched AURA lines score lower by design), every clip −16.0 to −16.3 LUFS with true peak at or
under −1.0 dBTP. Total narration in the ride: intro 74 s, briefings 37 / 28 / 22 s, outro 27 s.

To swap a voice: `render --guide-voice bm_george` (or `--aura-voice af_sarah`), `verify`, `install`, rebuild.

## Archival clips (lines 16 and 24)

JFK (16) and the Apollo 11 launch (24) are real recordings, prepared separately in `ArtSource/archival/` (sources,
licenses and the trim points are in `ArtSource/archival/SOURCES.md`). They are copied to
`Assets/_Project/Audio/Voice/vo_ride_16.wav` and `vo_ride_24.wav` with sidecars.

## Other engines

Any TTS can be plugged in with `--engine-module backend_x.py` (see the docstring at the top of `render_voices.py`).
`backend_qwen.py` and `QWEN_NOTES.md`, if present, are the Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign candidate.
