# Chamber 01 playtest

The observer stays silent and explains nothing. Five people who have never seen the game. Headset on. Use `playtest_log.txt` in the player's persistent data folder when a development or editor build is running.

Human sessions are **NOT MEASURED**. The results table is blank on purpose. Gate B was not passed by players.

## Setup

1. Open the project in Unity `6000.6.0f1` and load `Assets/Scenes/Level01_AwakeningGate.unity`.
2. Press Play. Sit at the authored rig. Do not walk the camera before the five-second test.
3. On a laptop, show the player-seat view for 5 seconds, hide it, and ask "what are you supposed to do?" Write their words.
4. Silent session, 5 minutes. Record the time to the first successful action, pauses longer than 10 seconds, unprompted retries, and timestamps of visible confusion or delight.
5. After, ask: "What does the dial do?" and "What happens if you make a wire thicker?"
6. For chamber 04 also ask: "Why couldn't one neuron do it?" and "What did the two neurons do?" Record their words.

## If a bar is missed

| What you saw | Look at |
|---|---|
| Did not know what to do in the first 20 s | Cold open, the one prompt, the glowing cable |
| Acted but did not notice the cause | Diagram, cards, and whether the evaluation arrived the same frame |
| Could not explain the dial | Term reveal, the bias wire, hover on a card |
| Did not retry | Payoff and the fail line |
| Looked bored | Hint, engage replay, or chambers 02 to 04 |
